## Who we are

- **Name:** PropFuel
- **What we do, in one sentence:** PropFuel shows associations what members
  actually need and makes it easy to keep them engaged.
- **Category:** Member Insights and Engagement Platform.
- **Tagline (everyday, front-facing):** Know and Grow Your Membership.
- **Big-picture line (vision and thought leadership only):** Make Membership
  Meaningful. Never use it as the everyday tagline.
- **Who we write for:** membership, marketing and engagement staff at
  professional associations and the AMCs that run them. Often a team of two
  to five people, measured on renewals, retention and engagement, with too
  much manual work and data scattered across an AMS, an email tool and
  spreadsheets.
- **What we sell:** a platform that asks members one question at a time,
  learns from every answer, and automates the next step: renewals,
  onboarding, win-back, engagement, data updates.

## What we sound like

- **Voice reference:** Tom Hanks. A trusted guide, not a corporate platform.
- **Traits:** approachable over elite, friendly over authoritative, simple
  over complex, bright over subtle, casual elegance over stiffness.
  Credibility plus warmth: trusted by executives, human for practitioners.
- **Words we use:** members, membership, renewals, engagement, onboarding,
  "ask, listen, act", conversation, signal, small team, "what members
  actually need".
- **Words we never use:** leverage, synergy, unlock, revolutionize,
  game-changer, cutting-edge, "in today's fast-paced world", delve, robust,
  seamless, empower (as filler), "AI-powered" as a headline claim.
- **Sentence length:** mixed. Short sentences to land a point, a longer one
  to explain. Plain words; make complex things simple.
- **Swearing:** no.
- **Emoji:** never in the company voice; at most one, rarely, on a personal
  profile.
- **Contractions:** yes.

## Our positions

1. Engagement isn't something you chase. It's something you sustain, because
   every interaction gives members a reason to stay.
2. Associations don't have a renewal problem. They have a "we don't know our
   members" problem, and renewals are where it shows up.
3. Most member communications only push out and bring nothing back. Every
   message should be a chance to listen.
4. A two-person membership team shouldn't need to work like a twenty-person
   one. Automation should do the translation work so people bring judgment.
5. Annual surveys are too slow. Ask one question at a time, all year.

## On Instagram specifically

- **Handle:** PropFuel's company account (check the exact handle in the OS
  `organic-content-engine` initiative before using it).
- **On camera:** warm, plain, a little dry. A membership person talking to
  another membership person, not a presenter. Real staff or real screens,
  never stock footage, never AI faces passed off as people.
- **Voiceover or to-camera:** either; to-camera wins when a teammate is
  willing. Faceless screen-recordings of the platform are fine.
- **Pace:** about 160 words a minute; `beats.py --wpm 160`.
- **Emoji in captions:** never on the company account; at most one, rarely,
  on a personal account.
- **Hashtags:** three, specific to association and membership work (e.g.
  #associationmanagement #membership #memberengagement), never generic
  growth tags.
- **The ask:** one per post. Save it, comment a keyword (only when there is a
  real guide to send), or follow. Never "book a demo" at the end of a reel.
- **Every line is said out loud.** If a teammate wouldn't say it on camera,
  rewrite it.
