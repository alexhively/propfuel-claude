To rebuild page 2 in HubSpot or Mailchimp, you'd need to set up:

  1. A custom field for every question
  2. A list or segment for every answer
  3. A workflow with branches for each email
  4. A landing page for every answer
  5. A follow-up question for the “no” answers
  6. A sync that writes answers back to your AMS
  7. Staff notifications for warm replies
  8. A rule that removes members when your AMS shows they renewed
