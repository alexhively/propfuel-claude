# Monthly SEO + GEO report

Build it as a workbook, one tab per section. Compute everything from Search Console, analytics and the AI-visibility tool; never leave placeholders. Without Search Console access, build from the last snapshot, label every number with its as-of date and put "Search Console access needed" at the top.

## Tabs
1. Summary: 5 bullets (what moved, what to double down on, what is stuck, GEO headline, the one ask).
2. Technical SEO (before/after): metric | before | now. From the audit crawl (titles, metas, canonicals, duplicates, 4xx, schema coverage, orphans, indexable pages, thin pages, slow pages).
3. KPI Scorecard: the 12 metrics below, previous month, current month, MoM %, growth to date, starting metric.
4. Keyword Tracker: Keyword | Target page | Vol | KD | Position now | Position last month | Change | Bucket (Top 10 / Page 2 / Page 3+ / Not ranking yet). Highlight position changes.
5. Priority Pages: per optimized or new page: impressions by month, non-brand impressions, clicks, average position, publish date.
6. AI Search: Google AI Overview / AI Mode impressions; AI-visibility visibility and citations per prompt cluster; AI referrals (analytics); branded prompt fact-check notes.
7. Work and Next Month: shipped (with links), in review, next month's pages, backlink placements, asks for the approver.
Plus raw tabs: Search Console monthly, keyword YoY.
Report on: clicks and performance per page; keyword positions and clicks per keyword; pages that improved or worsened most; trends; month-over-month comparison; GEO section.

## The 12 metrics (stage | metric | source)
Awareness: Total Impressions (GSC); Unbranded Impressions (GSC); Branded Impressions (GSC); Avg Keyword Position (GSC); # Keywords Ranked (volume tool or GSC query count); # Keywords in Top 10.
Consideration: Total Clicks (GSC); Unbranded Clicks (GSC); Branded Clicks (GSC); Avg CTR (GSC).
SEO hygiene: Domain Rating (volume/DR tool); Total Backlinks (same); # Indexed Pages (GSC).
Add when conversion tracking exists: organic sessions and organic conversions.
Branded query regex: set from the org's brand names (profile). Report a third bucket, "people" (team-member and celebrity-name queries), and keep it out of unbranded: it can be most of the unbranded clicks. Unbranded = everything else.

## Reading average position
- New pages enter at low positions (50 to 100) and temporarily raise the average. Filter by page or keyword; do not read the site-wide average.
- Always say whether a number is a monthly value or a 3-month average (an "8 vs 5" mismatch: the report said 8 keywords in the top 10, an average, while the filtered monthly sheet showed 5).
- Report non-brand average position separately from total.
- Use top-10 count and per-keyword movement as the headline, with the filter shown.
- Semantic variants can lag the head term; judge after a month of added content and internal links.

## Baselines
Starting metrics, targets and the last report's numbers live in the org profile and state doc. Compare to those; state whether progress is branded or unbranded.
