# Blog and template content

Why blogs: landing pages alone often do not move keywords. Blogs carry the informational cluster, and low-volume relevant posts earn topical relevance and AI coverage. The org profile holds the current triage and calendar.

## Content triage sheet
Header: "Content/SEO Strategy: which posts to write, in order".
Columns: Rank | Brief # | Blog post | Recommendation (Write now / Write next / Merge / GEO-support only) | Primary keyword | Searches/month | Difficulty | AI searches/month | Cluster searches/month | SERP fit (Strong / OK / Weak / Mismatch) | AI Overview? | Top 3 ranking now | Angle to win | Links to page | Client's order | Why | Approve (Approved / For review).

## Reasoning rules
1. Check SERP fit before committing: does the page type that ranks match a blog post?
2. Check whether an AI Overview shows and how many AI searches the topic gets (a topic with 880 searches and 2,868 AI searches is strong GEO value).
3. Cluster volume beats single-term volume: pick the primary keyword, count the cluster.
4. Do not target terms a landing page owns.
5. Consumer-intent head terms are used only at section level, not as the post's target.
6. Generic terms owned by big vendors are won on the niche angle (the qualifier from the profile).
7. Link every post to the landing page it supports (Links to page column), with a keyword anchor.
8. Keep low-volume relevant posts for topical relevance and GEO.
Without a volume tool: mark volume "unknown", use SERP fit and cluster judgment, say so in the sheet.

## Use-case blog kit: brief shape (the org's input to the writer)
One doc per campaign or use case:
1. One line.
2. Who it is for, when to run it, when not to.
3. How it works.
4. The questions to ask or steps to take, the answers, and why each works.
5. Follow-up flow.
6. Results: only from a named source, labeled "individual client results, not averages".
7. SEO and GEO brief: questions this answers (search and AI prompts, including "Build me a <thing> and write the emails"); suggested title; suggested H2s; FAQ ideas; tip (include a copy-and-paste version of any question list, since AI answers favor quotable lists); internal links; CTA (from the profile); sources.
The index lists "Facts to use (and only these)" from the profile's allow-list.
Goal: be the answer when someone asks ChatGPT or Google to "build me a <campaign> and write the emails". Templates are not proprietary, so be generous and specific. In-product templates or blueprints are the substance for template and GEO posts.
Example (PropFuel): renewals H2 pattern: Why most renewal emails get ignored; Start 90 days out; The five check-ins; What to do with each answer; What to measure; Example results.
