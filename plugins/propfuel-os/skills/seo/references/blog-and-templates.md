## Reasoning rules
1. Check SERP fit before committing: does the page type that ranks match a blog post?
2. Check whether an AI Overview shows and how many AI searches the topic gets (call for speakers: 880 searches, 2,868 AI searches = strong GEO value).
3. Cluster volume beats single-term volume: pick the primary keyword, count the cluster.
4. Do not target terms a landing page owns ("membership acquisition", "association membership").
5. Consumer-intent head terms (gym, fitness membership) are used only at section level, not as the post's target.
6. Generic terms owned by big vendors (NPS survey questions: Qualtrics, SurveyMonkey) are won on the association-specific angle ("member NPS").
7. Link every post to the landing page it supports (Links to page column), with a keyword anchor.
8. Keep low-volume relevant posts for topical relevance and GEO.
Without a volume tool: mark volume "unknown", use SERP fit and cluster judgment, say so in the sheet.

## Use-Case Blog Kit brief shape (PropFuel's input to the writer)
One doc per campaign use case:
1. One line.
2. Who it is for, when to run it, when not to.
3. How it works (short series of one-question check-ins; answer decides what they see next).
4. The questions to ask, the answers, and why each works.
5. Follow-up flow (what to do with each answer).
6. Results: Help Center results only, labeled "individual client results, not averages".
7. SEO and GEO brief: questions this answers (search and AI prompts, including "Build me a <campaign> and write the emails"); suggested title; suggested H2s; FAQ ideas; tip (include a copy-and-paste version of the questions, since AI answers favor quotable question lists); internal links (Help Center guide, use-case page, SMS, /book-a-demo); CTA ("See how 370+ associations use PropFuel to Know and Grow Your Membership. Book a demo."); sources.
The index lists "Facts to use (and only these)": 370+ associations, the tagline, 12% vs 0.98%, Help Center client results. Example H2 pattern (renewals): Why most renewal emails get ignored; Start 90 days out; The five check-ins; What to do with each answer; Renewing organizational memberships; Add a text or on-site reminder; What to measure; Example results from real associations.
Goal: be the answer when staff ask ChatGPT or Google to "build me a new member onboarding campaign and write the emails". The templates are not proprietary, so be generous and specific.
Blueprints (the in-platform campaign templates, e.g. member acquisition and renewal templates) are the substance for template and GEO posts. Source: the Help Center campaign guides category.
