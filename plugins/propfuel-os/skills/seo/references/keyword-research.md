# Keyword research

No volume/KD tool is connected (see SKILL.md section 5). Start from the existing 128-keyword list; for new terms mark volume and KD "unknown" and judge difficulty from the live SERP (who ranks, how big, whether they are association-specific).

## The 7-step method (Mamba's workbook checklist)
1. Pull keywords from competitors: top 20 keywords in positions 1-5 and 6-10 per competitor, site structure, keyword gap.
2. Add market keywords. Tag each "Improve Our Ranking" (PropFuel already ranks) or "Need To Rank".
3. Bucket by page or topic (parent-level).
4. Add data points: search volume, difficulty (KD), intent (Commercial / Informational), current position, assigned status.
5. Sort by volume within each bucket.
6. Remove irrelevant keywords (apply the ICP filter below; confirm borderline ones with Alex).
7. Cluster into Cluster Mapping and the Content Calendar: Primary vs Secondary keywords per page, with SV shown.

## Columns
- Raw Keyword List: Keyword | Monthly Volume | KD | CPC ($) | Search Intent | Funnel Stage (TOF/MOF/BOF) | Category (topic bucket, e.g. "Retention - Ideas").
- Raw Keyword List + Clustering (working version): Page | Keyword | Search Volume | Difficulty | Keyword Intent | Improve vs. Need ("Need To Rank" / "Improve Our Ranking") | Current Keyword Position | Assigned Status.
- Cluster Mapping: per parent topic, the landing page, pillar blog page, supporting posts. Drives the calendar.
- Header counts: Keyword Group | # Keywords | Total Volume | TOF | MOF | BOF.

## Easy-Win rule
KD <= 5 and volume >= 50. Mamba's 20 easy wins included: member engagement 400/4, member experience 300/0, member retention 300/2, member engagement strategy 200/2, membership marketing 200/2, member engagement platform 200/2, membership retention ideas 150/0, member survey 150/1, how to attract new members to an association 100/0, new member onboarding 100/0.

## Member vs association classification
- MEMBER variants: contains "member" or "membership" but not "association" (80 keywords, 6,230 vol in the Feb list).
- ASSOCIATION variants: contains "association" (47 keywords, 3,760 vol).
- OTHER: neither (e.g. "best ams software"). 128 keywords, 10,050 total volume.
- Association variants have higher KD and CPC and need page clusters. Member variants are easier and cheaper.
- Rule: keep the association variant as the focus keyword wherever one exists, even at 10 searches a month. Staff often drop the word "association" ("member engagement software"), so keep the broad variant as secondary or on the homepage.

## ICP filter
In: use case + "for associations" (member acquisition, retention, onboarding, engagement, experience, renewals, events); integration queries with an AMS name.
Out: AMS terms (association management software and its brands: the AMS is the CRM, PropFuel is not one, intent does not match, hardest fight); LMS ("lms for member engagement in associations" removed Sep 3); advocacy software; "membership benefits"; generic marketing tools (SMS marketing software, personalized email marketing: "we're never going to beat HubSpot on that, but we could beat them on email engagement for associations").
Allowed AMS angle: one "best AMS" post with PropFuel integration messaging (50+ AMS integrations).

## Funnel framework
- TOF (informational): blogs, guides, pillars. "how to reduce membership churn rate", "how to increase member engagement".
- MOF (problem-aware, comparison): comparison pages, solution guides, case studies. "best membership engagement software".
- BOF (high intent, commercial): product and use-case landing pages. "member engagement platform", "membership renewal automation".
Commercial and transactional terms go on landing pages; informational terms go to blogs.

## Prioritization order
1. ICP fit (association qualifier). 2. Intent (BOF money terms first). 3. Difficulty and CPC (CPC is a competitiveness and value signal; one AMS term at $16 CPC means many ads above organic). 4. Volume.
Keep tiny-volume association terms for topical and GEO relevance (e.g. "student membership benefits" about 10/mo). Target is top 3 for priority keywords. Strategy: many low-KD association-specific terms, not a few head terms (most target terms are under 100/mo with KD under 10).
