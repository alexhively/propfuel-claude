# Backlinks

Purpose: backlinks are a mechanical signal that points Google at the keywords and pages to prioritize. Relevance and established domains help. Content and links go live together. Once content is live, aim new links at keywords that are stagnating.

## Vendor criteria
In-market and in-language publishing; relevance to the org's field (SaaS, B2B, or the audience's vertical); high DR/DA; low spam score; real, existing traffic (check it, do not trust the vendor's number); topical fit.
Reject: link farms, no traffic, unrelated verticals, sites that publish anything for a fee without editing.

## Placement rules
- 2 anchors per placement, to 2 different money pages.
- Anchor = the target keyword of that page. Article topic = that page's use case.
- Targets: bottom-of-funnel and stagnating keywords. Prospect list and target pages go to the approver for sign-off before outreach.
- Vary anchors; do not repeat exact-match anchors to the same URL from many sites. Include some branded and URL anchors.
- Mapping examples live in the org profile.

## rel="sponsored"
Paid placements must carry rel="sponsored" (Google policy). Before any new paid placement: require the vendor to tag the link or choose an editorial placement. Check the live link's HTML (`curl` the page, grep the anchor).
Why: undisclosed paid links are link spam and risk a manual penalty, which costs more than the links earn.

## Better sources than paid batches
- The field's most credible trade media (not in vendor catalogs; cold outreach gets poor replies; route through a partnership conversation).
- Client and partner pages ("works with" and integration partner pages).
- Case-study co-publishing with named clients.
- Review sites for GEO (G2, Gartner-type directories) only where citation data shows LLMs pull from them. Reddit needs a careful subreddit-level approach. Wikipedia needs third-party non-promotional coverage.
- Subdomain-authority tricks (client subdomains pushing authority to the root) only apply to products that generate public client subdomains.

## Never
Gray-hat tactics (link trades at scale, PBNs, comment spam, "link drip" tricks from social reels): works short term, risks penalization. Disavow only genuinely toxic links, via a disavow file after the off-page audit.

## Monthly routine
1. Check last month's placements are live and the anchor and URL are right. 2. Pick this month's targets from the Keyword Tracker (Page 2 and stagnating terms). 3. Send the list to the approver. 4. Write the guest article (profile's spelling, facts allow-list, no unsourced stats). 5. Record in the tracker.
